### Pharmacy Sales & Profitability Analytics

A three-dashboard Power BI solution addressing commercial performance across countries, regions, pharmacies and products.

**Key insight:** sales volume alone does not guarantee profitability; margin and efficiency must be evaluated alongside revenue.

[Explore the project and its data model →](Pharmacy%20Sales%20%26%20Profitability%20Analytics/)

### Athlete Health & Injury Analytics

A multi-season Power BI solution analysing injury frequency, risk factors, severity and recovery outcomes across different sporting contexts.

**Key insight:** injury frequency and severity should be assessed together to better understand risk and its operational impact.

[Explore the project and its data model →](Athlete%20Health%20%26%20Injury%20Analytics/)

## 🛠️ Approach and technologies

- Power BI Desktop, Power Query and DAX
- Dimensional modelling (star schema)
- KPI definitions and consistent analytical measures
- Time intelligence and comparative analysis
- Decision-oriented visualisation, drill-down and stakeholder questions
- Documentation of data models, dashboards and key findings

The projects are based on data challenges and are independent of my professional work.
